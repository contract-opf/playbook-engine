Verdict. The one interview answer that becomes hard-binding — Q4 writes directly into signed floor.invariants — is collected by a plain prompt ("Which clause types are non-negotiable regardless of deal value?") that carries no disclosure of that weight, and SKILL.md explicitly forbids a second confirmation ("Do not ask the user to 'confirm' Q4 a second time").
